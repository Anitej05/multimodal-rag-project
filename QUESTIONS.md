# Multimodal Legal Case Analysis Questions

## 1. Case Summary
Summarize all facts of the dispute from the uploaded lease agreement, legal notice, audio recording, and supporting legal documents.

---

## 2. Legal Issue Identification
Identify the key legal issues in this case and specify which clauses or evidence are relevant to each issue.

---

## 3. Argument Extraction
Extract and compare the arguments supporting the landlord and the tenant separately.

---

## 4. Contractual Breach Analysis
Determine whether the delayed payment constitutes a contractual breach or whether there is evidence of waiver, extension, or mutual consent.

---

## 5. Evidence Validation
Identify contradictions, missing evidence, or weaknesses in either party’s claims that could affect the case outcome.

---

## 6. Final Legal Analysis
Generate a final case analysis including likely legal outcome, strongest evidence, applicable legal principles, and recommended next legal action for both parties.
